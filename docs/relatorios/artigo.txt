Water from air, without a compressor: how a calcium-chloride bed, a solenoid
and recycled lab glassware can harvest ~0.7 L of drinking water per night in
the Brazilian semi-arid region — and what 100,000 of these devices would mean.

1 · THE PROBLEM: THIRST ON TOP OF DROUGHT
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Over 30 million people live in the Brazilian semi-arid region (semiárido).
Groundwater is frequently saline; water trucks (caminhões-pipa) define household
hydric logistics; distributed solutions must be cheap, quiet, and maintainable.

Atmospheric Water Generators (AWG) exist — but almost all of them use a
vapor-compression refrigeration loop: a compressor, a condenser, an expansion
valve. That means high peak power, expensive parts, refrigerant gas, noise,
and a maintenance profile that a rural community cannot sustain.

▸ SEO keywords: atmospheric water generator, AWG, water from air, Brazilian
  semi-arid, drinking water scarcity, decentralized water supply.

[PT-BR] O compressor é o vilão: é o componente mais caro, mais barulhento e
mais difícil de consertar num AWG convencional. Nosso primeiro corte de projeto
foi eliminá-lo — e redesenhar todo o ciclo em volta dessa ausência.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2 · THE PHYSICS: BORROWING THE NIGHT (a 4-stage chemical-thermal cycle)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
The semi-arid night is the cheapest "water source" available: relative humidity
climbs from ~40 % (day) to ~65–70 % (night) while temperature drops. Our cycle
exploits that diurnal asymmetry with hygroscopic chemistry instead of cold:

▸ STAGE 1 — NOCTURNAL SORPTION (INTAKE)
  Fans pull night air through a calcium-chloride (CaCl₂) bed. The salt
  chemically retains water (deliquescence → CaCl₂·nH₂O), kinetically modeled
  as 1st-order saturation: m(t) = M·q·(1 − e^(−k·t)), capped at M·q ≈ 2 kg.

▸ STAGE 2 — ISOLATION (servo damper)
  At dawn, a servo motor closes the fresh-air inlet and opens the path to the
  distillation glassware duct. No back-contamination, no air short-circuit.

▸ STAGE 3 — SOLAR-SYNCHRONOUS REGENERATION (REGEN)
  A solenoid heater drives the wet salt to ~120 °C (thermostat with ±2 °C
  hysteresis). Water is released as pure vapor — the salt is not consumed, it
  is a reversible chemical "sponge".

▸ STAGE 4 — DISTILLATION + POST-TREATMENT → POTABLE BASIN
  Vapor condenses in recycled laboratory glassware (η ≈ 0.92). The distillate
  then passes through a mineralizing filter (Ca²⁺ 45 mg/L, Mg²⁺ 18 mg/L —
  distilled water alone tastes flat and is not an ideal drinking profile) and
  a UV-C lamp (254 nm, ≥ 40 mJ/cm²) before storage in a 2 L basin with a tap.

The whole chain is compressor-free, refrigerant-free, and pump-free.

[PT-BR] A sacada é usar o Ritmo do dia: o sertão "deseca" de dia e "umedece"
à noite. A máquina dorme com o clima: captura à noite, destila com o sol.
Química barata no lugar de mecânica cara.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3 · THE ENGINEERING: ONE CORE, MANY BODIES (C++23 · Python · IoT)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
We built a single deterministic simulation core (header-only C++23) and made
every consumer speak to the same physics:

▸ Native core (tupan_core.hpp): Magnus psychrometrics, sorption kinetics,
  energy-limited distillation, post-treatment stage; flat-JSON constants
  loader (O(n) time, O(1) extra memory — no DOM tree); xorshift64* PRNG;
  Monte Carlo engine parallelized with std::jthread over disjoint ranges.

▸ ML layer: ridge regression (degree 2) trained in streaming fashion on
  XᵀX/XᵀY accumulators — 500 samples never fully materialize in RAM. We added
  3 "engineering features" (vapor content ρ_v(T)·UR, bed saturation ratio,
  release fraction) that give the polynomial the kinks of the real physics.
  Result: R² = 0.991, RMSE = 0.064 L — physics parity without neural nets.

▸ Data pipeline: pandas + scikit-learn + PyTorch over 14,616 hourly ERA5/
  Open-Meteo records for Petrolina-PE. Climate finding: mean nocturnal
  conditions of 68.6 % RH at 24.6 °C → projected 593.8 L/year (≈ 0.97 L/day
  average, with strong seasonality).

▸ Firmware (Arduino Mega 2560, C++20): MVC + finite state machine
  (OCIOSO → INTAKE → REGEN → DESTIL → CHEIO/ERRO), zero dynamic Strings
  (everything F()/PROGMEM), LOG_* console telemetry for every FSM transition,
  hysteresis thermostat, servo damper control.

▸ Web simulator: Flask + pybind11 binding of the native core (Python fallback
  mirroring the exact formulas), animated canvas showing air particles → bed →
  vapor → glassware → basin. Parity test: native 0.689 L vs web 0.691 L
  per identical cycle.

▸ Interactive studio: the same core drives a Lua-defined UI (sol2) built with
  Dear ImGui + OpenGL 3.3 over a small hand-rolled linear-algebra layer
  (`Real=float`, union-backed Mat4). Menus, panels and the 3D scene live in a
  JSON-like Lua file (assets/studio.lua) — reloadable without recompiling. A
  headless runner (luaaa) exposes the physics to scripts as `tupan.*`.

▸ Companion app mockups: 6 screens (dashboard, cycle timeline, sensors,
  controls, history, Bluetooth/MQTT pairing) rendered from executable HTML
  via headless Chromium — the mockups are specs, not drawings.

[PT-BR] Tudo nasce de um único núcleo físico: o CLI em C++, o studio
interativo em Lua/ImGui/OpenGL, o simulador web e o firmware contam a MESMA
história, com os MESMOS números. Auditoria de 35 testes C++ + 21 testes Python,
todos passando. Entrega em Docker multi-stage com pipeline Jenkins → Kubernetes/VPS.

4 · THE ENERGY ECONOMICS (Brazilian average tariffs)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Per standard cycle (8 h night + 6 h day, ERA5-average conditions):

▸ Solenoid (250 W × 6 h)        1.50 kWh   (89 % of the cycle)
▸ Fans (7.5 W × 8 h)            0.06 kWh
▸ Electronics 24 h (5 W)        0.12 kWh
▸ UV-C (6 W × 0.5 h)            0.003 kWh
▸ TOTAL                         ≈ 1.68 kWh  →  0.68 L potable  →  2.48 kWh/L

Using the Brazilian average residential tariff (premise R$ 0.95/kWh billed,
including taxes; ANEEL's tariff ranking spans ~R$ 0.80–1.20/kWh):

▸ Green flag year:            ≈ R$ 584 / year  → R$ 2.36 per liter
▸ Red flag P2 year:           ≈ R$ 588 / year  → R$ 2.38 per liter
▸ With Tarifa Social (65 % discount — 51 kWh/month fits the ≤ 80 kWh tier):
                              ≈ R$ 204 / year  → R$ 0.83 per liter

Honest benchmark: a 20 L bottled-water jug costs ≈ R$ 0.60/L in bulk retail.
Tupan is NOT cheaper than piped/bottled water where those exist reliably.
Its value proposition is autonomy: no truck schedule, no supply chain, no
salinity. And the energy story improves dramatically under photovoltaic
assistance, where the 1.5 kWh day-stage is a perfect solar load.

[PT-BR] Transparência: o Tupan não compete com a Sabesp nem com o galão de
20 litros — ele compete com a INCERTEZA. Quando o caminhão-pipa não vem, o
litro de água não tem preço; quando vem, custa gasolina, fila e tempo.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5 · THE SCALE TEST: WHAT IF 100,000 TUPANS WERE DEPLOYED? (a warning)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Modeling mass adoption is a duty, not a marketing exercise. Consider a
scenario of N = 100,000 devices running 365 cycles/year:

▸ ANNUAL WATER:        ~24.7 ML/yr (0.68 L × 365 × 100k) — meaningful for
                       ~100k people at drinking/minimum-hygiene rates, but
                       statistically IRRELEVANT to any aquifer or river.

▸ ANNUAL ENERGY:       ~61 GWh/yr (1.68 kWh × 365 × 100k).
                       ⚠️ WARNING 1 — GRID: that is the annual consumption of
                       ~11,000 Brazilian households. If deployed as a subsidy
                       program on the conventional grid, it adds peak-evening
                       demand in poor municipalities. Mitigation is structural,
                       not optional: PV panel + small battery as the DEFAULT
                       configuration, turning the day-stage solenoid into a
                       midday solar load.

▸ SALT & MATERIALS:    CaCl₂ is not consumed but degrades with dust and
                       impurities. ⚠️ WARNING 2 — WASTE: 100k devices ×
                       ~2 kg bed replaced every 2–3 years = 70–100 t of spent
                       salt over a decade. Untreated dumping would salinize
                       soils — precisely the problem we are solving. A take-
                       back/regeneration program must be part of any public
                       policy, not an afterthought. (Recovery by simple re-
                       drying at > 150 °C re-activates most of the bed mass.)

▸ GLASSWARE & FILTERS: recycled lab glassware is elegant at 1 unit and a
                       logistics nightmare at 100k. Local fabrication (3D-
                       printed PLA housings, standardized borosilicate parts)
                       and a UV-lamp replacement schedule (6 W lamps degrade
                       below dose in ~9,000 h) are prerequisites.

▸ BEHAVIORAL RISK:     ⚠️ WARNING 3 — TRUST: distilled-then-mineralized water
                       is safe, but communities will only adopt it with local
                       water-quality testing and visible maintenance culture.
                       One contaminated unit in one village can kill the
                       program's credibility region-wide.

▸ ECONOMIC EQUITY:     ⚠️ WARNING 4 — TARIFF REGRESSION: without Tarifa Social
                       enrollment or PV support, R$ 2.4/L is regressive — it
                       would be the poorest households paying the most per
                       liter of drinking water in Brazil. Any deployment policy
                       must couple the device to TSEE registration and/or
                       off-grid energy.

[PT-BR] Advertência central: escala inverte virtudes. Um dispositivo que é
"limpo" vira 100 t de sal gasto; um ciclo que é "barato para uma família"
vira 61 GWh de demanda. A conclusão do projeto é sócio-técnica: solarização e
logística reversa do sal não são acessórios verdes, são condições de contorno
para que o Tupan não crie o problema que pretende resolver.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6 · COMPLIANCE SNAPSHOT (why this matters beyond the prototype)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
A potable-water device in Brazil lives under a dense regulatory stack, and the
project was designed with that stack in mind from day one:

▸ INMETRO — Portaria 344/2017 (water treatment systems for human consumption):
  defines the certification route for household water treatment/dispensing
  equipment, including UV-based systems; a productized Tupan would need type
  approval by an accredited OPC/RT (testing of microbiological and physical-
  chemical performance across the declared operating range).

▸ ANVISA — consolidated ordinances on drinking-water potability (Portaria de
  Consolidação nº 5, Anexo XX; reference parameters of the old Portaria
  2.914/2011): our post-treatment target profile (Ca 45 mg/L, Mg 18 mg/L,
  UV-C dose ≥ 40 mJ/cm²) is engineered against those parameter tables.

▸ ABNT NBR 16098 / NBR 15176: household point-of-use treatment devices and
  water quality sampling — the test protocol skeleton for the academic report.

▸ LGPD (Lei 13.709/2018): the companion app pairs over Bluetooth/MQTT with a
  unique device ID; no personal data is required to operate the machine, and
  telemetry is stored locally by design (privacy as architecture, not policy).

▸ Energy: the device operates within ANEEL's tariffed residential class and
  inside the Tarifa Social eligibility envelope (≤ 80 kWh/month) — a deliberate
  design constraint, not a coincidence.

[PT-BR] Conformidade não é burocracia: é o que separa "um experimento de
laboratório" de "um aparelho de água potável". O caminho INMETRO (Portaria
344) é caro para um protótipo acadêmico — mas o projeto já nasce com a
arquitetura de conformidade: UV dosimétrica, perfil mineral documentado,
registro auditável de cada ciclo.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7 · WHAT WE MEASURED, WHAT WE LEARNED (academic takeaways)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
▸ Physics beats vibes: the sorption ceiling (M·q) and the energy limit of the
  solenoid are the two REAL constraints; everything else is second-order.
  The ML model only became accurate (R² 0.877 → 0.991) after we injected the
  two kinks of the physics as features — data cannot replace a mechanism.

▸ The climate is the design document: 14,616 h of ERA5 data for ONE city
  changed the machine (8 h night window, 120 °C regeneration, 2 L basin).
  Site-specificity is a feature of AWG engineering, not a limitation.

▸ Software architecture is sustainability: one core, five consumers, two
  test suites, all deterministic and auditable. Reproducibility is the only
  honest form of "trust me" an academic project can offer.

▸ The device is a policy object: energy source, salt logistics and tariff
  class decide its social impact more than its airflow does. Engineering
  education should treat INMETRO/ANVISA/ANEEL as part of the CAD assembly.

[PT-BR] Lição para colegas de engenharia: o protótipo é a parte fácil.
A parte difícil — e que a disciplina de IoT & PLCs nos obrigou a encarar —
é o entorno: dados climáticos reais, norma técnica, tarifa de energia e
comunidade que vai usar a coisa.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8 · THE INTERACTIVE STUDIO (and how the whole thing ships)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
A physics core is only as convincing as the tools around it — so we built an
interactive studio where the interface itself is a Lua script.

▸ The interface is data: menus, panels and the 3D scene are declared in a
  JSON-like Lua table (assets/studio.lua) and read with sol2. Change the .lua
  and reload — no recompilation. A menu item literally calls "Reload DSL".

▸ 3D without an engine: GLFW + Dear ImGui + OpenGL 3.3 over a hand-rolled
  linear-algebra layer (Real = float, and a column-major Mat4 whose `union`
  exposes both float[16] and Vec4 columns). Fans spin during sorption, the coil
  glows in regeneration, and the basin level rises with the liters the core
  actually computes.

▸ Binding in both directions: sol2 reads the declarative UI; luaaa (a
  single-header C++→Lua binder) exposes the physics as `tupan.*`, so a headless
  script can sweep humidity and print liters/kWh without opening a window.

▸ Delivery is code too: a multi-stage Docker image (core CLI + gunicorn web
  with a /health probe) and a Jenkinsfile that builds, tests, regenerates all
  media, pushes, and deploys — to Kubernetes (Deployment/Service/Ingress/HPA,
  runAsNonRoot, readiness/liveness probes) or to a plain VPS (systemd + nginx).

[PT-BR] A interface virou script: mudar um painel ou a cena 3D é editar um .lua
e recarregar. O mesmo núcleo alimenta CLI, studio, web, app e firmware — há um
só lugar para consertar a verdade. E o pipeline de entrega é reprodutível de
ponta a ponta, do build ao deploy.
